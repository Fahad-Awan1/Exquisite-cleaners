Questions

Good to know.

Which areas do you cover?

We’re based in Accra and serve Greater Accra. Message us if you’re elsewhere in Ghana and we’ll let you know what we can arrange.

I live abroad. Can you look after my parents or property?

Yes, that’s exactly what Exquisite Family Care and our Airbnb management service are for. We act as your trusted people on the ground and send regular updates and photographs.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

No. Many clients arrange key access or have a family member let us in. We agree access details with you when you book.

Can I pay in pounds or dollars?

Prices on the site can be shown in cedis, pounds or dollars. We share payment options, including options for clients abroad, when we confirm your booking.

Do you offer contracts for businesses?

Yes. We focus on long-term cleaning contracts for offices, restaurants, schools, retail spaces and commercial buildings, with a dedicated team.
