Host questions

Hosting, answered.

I live outside Ghana. Can you run everything for me?

Yes. This service is designed for owners abroad. We handle the turnovers, guests and maintenance, and keep you updated with photo reports.

How do you know when a guest checks out?

We agree a simple way to share your booking calendar, so our team is scheduled automatically around every stay.

What happens if something breaks?

We inspect the property after every stay, report any damage with photos and coordinate repairs through our trusted maintenance providers once you approve.

Do you supply the linen and toiletries?

We can launder your own linen or help you set up a full linen and essentials supply. We’ll recommend what works best for your property.
